## Description

Call Recorder saves a video call to a single file on your own computer. It records the call tab (everyone you hear and see) and your microphone together, cleans up your voice, and keeps the file small.

Nothing leaves your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no upload. Recordings go to your Downloads folder, or to a folder you choose.

What you get
• One file with the video of the tab, the other people's voices and yours.
• Your voice is cleaned with a small noise reduction model that runs on your computer.
• Small files: video is capped at 720p and 15 frames per second, the encoder is chosen to be light on your processor, and speech gets enough bits to stay clear.
• Live level meters show that your voice and the call are being heard, and a warning appears if a recording came out silent.
• Recording keeps going when you close the popup, and a long call never fills your memory, because the file is written to disk as it records.
• A library page lists your recordings with the time, length, size and the tab title, so you can find the meeting later.
• If the browser closes in the middle of a call, the recording is kept and you can save it from the library.

Works with web calls in Chrome
• Screen and tab sharing: when someone shares in the call on Google Meet or Konvene, the sound of the share is recorded too.
• Konvene: everyone in the call sees a badge with your name while you record.
• Google Meet and any other call in a browser tab: the extension asks you to confirm you have told everyone before it starts.

Please record responsibly. In many places you need everyone's consent to record a call. Call Recorder makes that visible, but the responsibility is yours.

Call Recorder is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Google or any call service named above.

Privacy: the call tab and microphone are recorded only after you press Start, and only to a file on your computer. Read the full policy at the privacy policy link on this page.
